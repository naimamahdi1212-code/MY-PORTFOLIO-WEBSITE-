About Me

Where the charts meet the classroom

I'm a forex trader with 6 years of experience reading price action, managing risk, and navigating the currency markets. Over the years, trading stopped being just my job — it became something I wanted to hand down.

That's why I founded Naima's School, a learning space where I teach fellow Somali girls and women forex and general trading concepts — from reading candlestick charts to building a full trading strategy and managing risk like a professional.

When I'm not analyzing charts, I'm building lessons, running live classes, and watching my students place their first confident trades.
