About Me

Where logic meets creativity

I'm a software developer and graphic designer who believes the best digital products are built at the intersection of clean engineering and thoughtful design. I spend my days writing maintainable code and my nights sketching layouts, type systems, and brand concepts.

My path started in visual design, which taught me to think in hierarchy, contrast, and story. Development gave me the tools to bring those ideas to life — fully interactive, responsive, and built to last. Today I move fluidly between Figma and my code editor, often on the same project.

When I'm not designing or building, I'm mentoring junior developers, exploring new design trends, or sketching out my next side project.
